## 📌 Overview

A single-screen Excel dashboard tracking McDonald's sales performance across Latin America — built to practice KPI visualization beyond standard bar/pie charts. This one uses **donut gauge charts** for target tracking, a **radar chart** for customer satisfaction across five dimensions, and a **year-over-year trend line** comparing 2025 vs 2026 monthly sales.

## 🔍 What the Dashboard Shows

**KPI Gauges** — Three donut-style progress gauges tracking performance against target: Sales at **85%**, Profit at **89%**, and Customers at **87%** of goal — all three metrics are tracking close together, none dramatically ahead or behind.

**Customer Satisfaction (Radar Chart)** — Breaks satisfaction into five dimensions: **Availability (95%)** and **Hygiene (93%)** score the highest, while **Service (53%)** and **Speed (54%)** are the clear weak points — customers rate the product and cleanliness highly, but the actual service experience and speed lag well behind. This is the kind of insight a single satisfaction score would hide.

**Sales Trend 2025 vs 2026** — 2026 tracks above 2025 for most of the first half of the year, but both years dip mid-year (around July–August) before recovering. 2026 shows a stronger, more consistent climb into Q4 compared to 2025's flatter finish.

**Sales By Country** — **Argentina dominates** with 953 (more than double the next country), followed by Brazil (553), Ecuador (445), Peru (425), and Colombia (432). Chile is the smallest market at 254.
